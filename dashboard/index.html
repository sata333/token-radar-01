How This Radar Ranks Pre-TGE Launches

All metrics are computed from a frozen DefiLlama snapshot (2026-11-14, 758 protocols with TVL ≥ $5M). For each protocol lacking a recorded token, we estimate Conditional Implied FDV by multiplying TVL by its category's tokenized-peer median mcap/TVL (with fee-take-rate haircuts for thin categories such as Risk Curators at 0.045× and Onchain Capital Allocators at 0.108×), applying a conservative 1.60× circulating-to-FDV lift, and adjusting for 7-day TVL momentum (0.80×–1.25×), protocol age maturity (0.85×–1.05×), and chain liquidity quality/breadth (0.80×–1.25×). We then multiply Conditional Implied FDV by Launch Likelihood (P_launch, 2%–95%) to rank the board by Expected Launch Value (EV_FDV). Protocols that are already live stablecoins, modules of parent-tokenized chains, or non-tokenizable public goods are transparently retained with TOKEN TRAP or NO-TGE flags rather than silently deleted. Not financial advice; implied valuations are model estimates, not price targets.

The formula

Implied FDV = TVL × M_base(category) × 1.60 × F_growth(7d) × F_age(listed_at) × F_chain(primary chains)
EV = Implied FDV × P_launch  ·  P_launch = curated override where researched, else clamp(P_cat + Δ_age + Δ_eco + Δ_mom)

Reading the board

The 40 watchlist protocols above the $50M floor carry hand-researched Curated Intel: verified launch likelihoods, trap audits and bespoke farm plans. Rows beyond the watchlist are Auto-Scored by the same formula from snapshot fields only, with templated plans — they are labelled, never passed off as hand-audited. Chain footprint is scored on a protocol's three primary deployments; long-tail chain listings are treated as bridge dust, not liquidity.
